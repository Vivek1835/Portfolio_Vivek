How to make chocolate

Chocolate is made from cocoa beans, which are harvested from the cacao tree. The beans are fermented, dried, roasted, and ground into a paste called chocolate liquor. This liquor can be further processed to separate cocoa solids and cocoa butter. To make chocolate, the chocolate liquor is mixed with sugar, milk (for milk chocolate), and other ingredients, then refined and conched to develop a smooth texture and rich flavor. Finally, the mixture is tempered and molded into bars or other shapes.
